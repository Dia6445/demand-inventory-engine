# Business findings

## Growth
- Monthly revenue grew from about 120k (Jan 2017) to about 660k (Oct 2017), then stayed around 850k to 990k in 2018.
- Demand is not stable over time, so a model trained on 2017 will see a very different level in 2018.

## Black Friday
- Nov 2017 revenue was 1,003,862 vs 660,180 in Oct 2017 (about 52% higher).
- Average delivery time rose from 11.9 to 15.2 days in the same month.

## Categories
- Top revenue: health_beauty, watches_gifts, bed_bath_table.
- bed_bath_table sells the most units (11,089) but is only third in revenue.
- Niche categories (food, construction_tools, art) have the most unstable weekly demand (variability 0.8 to 1.05) and sell 2 to 14 units per week.

## Products
- The best product sold 527 units in 20 months, about 6 per week.
- Product-level forecasting is hard, so the main forecast is at category level.

## Delivery
- Average delivery time: about 11 days in mid-2017, 15 to 17 days in Nov 2017 to Mar 2018, 7.7 days in Aug 2018.
- Orders without a delivery date are excluded, so recent months may look better than they are.

## Sellers
- The top seller has 1.7% of revenue and the top 10 about 13%. No single seller dominates.
